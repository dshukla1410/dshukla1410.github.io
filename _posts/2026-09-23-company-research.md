23 and me

Who were the founders? 
Anne Wojcicki(served as longtime ceo), Linda Avey (left in 2009 but remained a board member until 2011), and Paul Cusenza (was a founder in the very early stages but left before it was released)

When the company was founded? 
April 2006

How much was their initial investment? 
$14 million Series A funding round which was famously known for being backed by Google along with personal backing from Anne Wojcicki 

What is the current revenue?
Due to falling demand in at-home DNA testing kits, their annual revenue as of 2025 is only around $170 million. The company's final public earnings reports before transitioning out of the public stock market showed a 37% year-over-year drop to $25.4 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026.

What is their main business focus?
Their main business focus is selling saliva based DNA kits that deliver personalized ancestry directly to the consumer. Additionally they are using their massive, crowdsourced database of consented genetic profiles to discover novel drug targets and license intellectual property to pharmaceutical giants.

What is the future success of the company?
The company's future is at high stakes following their filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in early 2025 due to their inability to find a profitable path as a public entity. Following intense bidding wars, CEO Anne Wojcicki bought back the company's assets for $305 million in mid-2025, taking it private under a newly formed entity called the TTAM Research Institute. Its long-term viability hinges on a major "nonprofit moonshot" strategy aimed at scaling up to 100 million DNA users while shifting focus toward clinical medical research, data safety guarantees, and deep partnerships in personalized medicine.
