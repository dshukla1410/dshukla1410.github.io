What is breast cancer?
Breast cancer is a disease where cells in the breast grow out of control and form a tumor.

How frequent is it?
Breast cancer is one of the most common types of cancers in women. It accounts for about 1 in 3 women cancers in women eah year. According to the American Cancer Society's 2026 estimates, about 321,910 new cases of invasive breast cancer will be diagnosed in women.

How is it typically identified?
It is typically detected through routine screening tests, physical exams, or when a person notices physical symptoms like a new lump.

What factors heighten risk?
The biggest risk factor is being an older woman; most cases are diagnosed in individuals age 55 or older or those with family history.

What is the staging system used?
The staging system most often used in breast cancer is the American Joint Committee on Cancer TNM system which groups cancers into stages 0 to 4. Doctors also often use molecular subtypes using key proteins and gentic markers found on the cancer cell.

What are current possible treatments?
Current available treatments include for breast cancer include surgery, radiation, chemotherapy, hormone therapy, targeted drugs, and immunotherapy based on the cancer's stage.

What is the life expectancy?
Overall, the 5-year survival rate for breast cancer combined over al stages is around 92%.
